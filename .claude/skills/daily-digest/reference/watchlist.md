## AI Topic Filters

### 🔥 Must Include (Primary — score 7-10)
- New AI model releases and benchmarks (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral, etc.)
- AI agent frameworks and workflows (MCP, LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, etc.)
- AI coding tools and IDE integrations (Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code, Copilot, v0, bolt, etc.)
- AI product launches and demos
- Open source model weights, fine-tuning, LoRA, quantization
- RAG, embeddings, vector DB, knowledge base
- AI infrastructure (training, inference, serving, edge deployment)
- Prompt engineering, system prompts, jailbreak research
- Multimodal AI (vision, TTS, STT, video gen, image gen, music gen)
- AI API pricing changes, rate limits, new features
- Local LLM (Ollama, llama.cpp, vLLM, etc.)
- AI browser automation, web agents, computer use

### 📰 Include if Substantial (Secondary — score 4-6)
- AI funding rounds, acquisitions, company news (focus on technical/product aspects)
- AI safety research, alignment discussions (technical implementation only)
- AI's impact on specific industries (education, healthcare, legal, creative) with concrete use cases
- Technical opinions and predictions about AI development trends
- AI hardware news (GPUs, TPUs, custom chips, Apple Silicon ML)
- AI research papers worth noting

### ❌ Exclude (score 0-3)
- Personal life posts unrelated to AI/tech
- Food, travel, selfies
- Pure memes without AI substance
- Marketing spam, affiliate links
- Crypto/Web3 unless directly AI-related

### 🚫 Political/Sensitive Topics — Hard Exclude (score 0)
Even if AI-related, **completely exclude** the following:
- **China/US/International politics**: Government policy, regulatory frameworks, geopolitical competition, national AI strategies
- **Political figures**: Unless announcing specific technical products (e.g., "Biden announces new AI model" → exclude; "OpenAI launches new API" → include even if politician mentioned)
- **Government relations**: Trade restrictions, sanctions, export controls, diplomatic tensions
- **Corporate politics**: Executive power struggles, governance disputes, internal company conflicts unrelated to products
- **Social movements**: Political protests, activism, ideological debates (unless demonstrating specific AI technical applications)

✅ **Allowed if technical substance exists**:
- AI ethics research with concrete technical solutions (bias detection algorithms, fairness metrics)
- AI safety mechanisms and implementation details
- Industry-specific AI applications (healthcare diagnosis AI, educational AI tutors, legal document analysis)
- Technical compliance requirements (GDPR data handling, API terms changes)
